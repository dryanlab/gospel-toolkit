## 正文（中文）

我是摩西。弟兄姐妹，出埃及记第二十六章看起来是一份冗长的建筑清单，但当我在西奈山上听神一句一句把这些样式启示给我，我心里清楚，这不是图纸，这是福音。神要在罪人的营中"支搭帐棚"，祂要降卑下来与人同住。请你不要把这章当成博物馆里的工艺品介绍，要把它当成神亲手画的基督画像。

**四层罩棚，由内向外看基督的层次（第1-14节）**

帐幕有四层覆盖。最里面是十幅细麻幔子，绣着蓝色、紫色、朱红色线和基路伯。祭司在圣所里抬头一看，看见的是天上圣所的荣耀，是基路伯环绕的宝座。弟兄姐妹，这是基督的荣耀本相，约翰福音一章十四节说"我们见过他的荣光，正是父独生子的荣光"，那荣光只有进到幕里的祭司才看得见。

往外一层是十一幅山羊毛幔子。山羊在利未记里是赎罪祭的牲畜，山羊毛粗糙，毫无装饰。再往外是公羊皮染红的盖，红色是血色，是亚伯拉罕献以撒时神预备的那只公羊（创22:13）的影子。最外面是海狗皮，又厚又黑，远远看去帐幕是丑陋的，不引人注意的。

弟兄姐妹，这四层罩棚就是基督。以赛亚书五十三章说祂"无佳形美容""无美貌使我们羡慕祂"，那是最外面的海狗皮，世人看祂只是拿撒勒木匠的儿子，是被人厌弃、被人藐视的那一位。再往里一层是公羊皮染红，那是祂被钉十字架时流出的宝血，是祂在客西马尼园汗如血滴的痛苦。再往里是山羊毛，那是祂担当我们罪孽时的赎罪祭身份，是以赛亚书五十三章"祂被欺压，在受苦的时候却不开口；祂像羊羔被牵到宰杀之地"。最里面才是细麻绣金的荣光，是父怀里的独生子，是道与神同在的那一位，是登山变像时摩西和以利亚所见的那位荣耀的主。世人只看到最外面那层，但凭信进到幕里的人，才看见祂里面的荣耀。这就是为什么从外向内是苦难，从内向外是荣耀，每一层都是基督。

**圣所与至圣所，幔子的分隔（第31-35节）**

第三十一节神吩咐我做一面幔子，悬挂在四根包金的柱子上，"幔子要将圣所和至圣所隔开"。这面幔子和最外的门帘不同，它绣着基路伯，因为它是把守神宝座的。约柜在幔子里面，施恩座在幔子里面，神的荣光在幔子里面。我哥哥亚伦，作大祭司，一年只能在赎罪日进去一次，还必须带着血。没有血，进去就是死。拿答和亚比户献凡火被烧死（利10:1-2），就是因为忽视了这道分隔。

弟兄姐妹，这面幔子说出一个残酷的事实，亚当犯罪以后，人与神之间隔着一道幕。基路伯的火焰剑曾把人挡在伊甸园外（创3:24），如今幔子上同样绣着基路伯，把人挡在至圣所外。我们的罪让我们进不去。律法、礼仪、圣殿，没有一样能让一个普通的以色列人走进至圣所去与神面对面。

但是马太福音二十七章五十一节告诉我们，主耶稣在十字架上断气的那一刻，圣殿里这面幔子"从上到下裂为两半"。请你听清楚，是从上到下，不是从下到上，不是人撕的，是神亲手撕的。希伯来书十章二十节解经："是藉着他给我们开了一条又新又活的路，从幔子经过，这幔子就是他的身体。"基督的身体在十架上被撕裂，那一刻幔子也被撕裂，路就开了。如今你不必等到赎罪日，不必透过大祭司，你可以"坦然无惧地来到施恩的宝座前"（来4:16）。

**帐幕本身，神的儿子住在我们中间**

弟兄姐妹，整个第二十六章的核心不是材料尺寸，是一个动词，神要"住"在人中间。希伯来文是 שכן（shakan），后来犹太人称神的同在为"舍金拿"（Shekinah）。约翰福音一章十四节希腊文用了一个奇特的词 ἐσκήνωσεν，字面意思是"支搭了帐棚"，"道成了肉身，在我们中间支搭了帐棚"。约翰是故意用这个词的，他要让所有读旧约的犹太人立刻想起这章经文。

我在旷野指挥百姓搭起的这个帐幕，不过是个影子。真正的帐幕是基督的身体。神不是住在皂荚木和金子里，神是住在那位道成肉身的儿子里。如今这位基督借着圣灵住在每个信徒里，住在教会里。亲爱的弟兄姐妹，你就是神的殿，神的灵住在你里面（林前3:16）。这章经文不是关于一座古代的帐棚，是关于你。

## Content (English)

I am Moses. Brothers and sisters, Exodus 26 reads at first like a long building list, but as the LORD spoke each measurement to me on Sinai, I understood what He was doing. This is not a blueprint. This is a portrait of Christ, painted by God in cloth and wood and gold. Do not treat this chapter as an antiquarian curiosity. Treat it as the gospel.

**Four Coverings, Christ Seen From Inside Out (vv. 1-14)**

The Tabernacle had four layers. The innermost was ten curtains of fine twined linen, embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet, and with cherubim. The priest serving inside looked up and saw the glory of the heavenly throne. John 1:14 says, "we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father." That glory was visible only to those who entered.

The second layer was eleven curtains of goats' hair. The goat is the sin-offering animal of Leviticus, rough and unadorned. The third was rams' skins dyed red, the color of blood, an echo of the ram caught in the thicket when Abraham bound Isaac (Gen. 22:13). The outermost was sea cow hides, thick and dark. From a distance the Tabernacle looked plain, even ugly.

Brothers and sisters, these four layers are Christ. Isaiah 53 says, "he hath no form nor comeliness; and when we shall see him, there is no beauty that we should desire him." That is the outer sea cow hide. The world saw only the carpenter's son from Nazareth. The next layer was the red rams' skin, His blood shed on Calvary. Beneath that the goats' hair, His role as the sin-offering "led as a lamb to the slaughter." Only at the center is the linen of glory, the Only Begotten in the bosom of the Father. The world sees the outside; faith enters and beholds the glory within. From outside in is suffering; from inside out is glory. Every layer is Christ.

**The Veil, Holy Place and Most Holy Place (vv. 31-35)**

The LORD commanded me to make a veil hung on four pillars overlaid with gold, "and the vail shall divide unto you between the holy place and the most holy." This veil was embroidered with cherubim, for it guarded the throne. Behind it lay the ark, the mercy seat, and the glory. My brother Aaron, as High Priest, could enter only once a year, on the Day of Atonement, and never without blood. Nadab and Abihu died for ignoring this separation (Lev. 10:1-2).

The cherubim on the veil echo the cherubim with flaming sword stationed at Eden's gate (Gen. 3:24). Sin had closed the way. No ordinary Israelite could ever stand before the LORD face to face. But Matthew 27:51 tells us that at the moment Jesus gave up His spirit, the Temple veil "was rent in twain from the top to the bottom." From the top, not the bottom. Not torn by man. Torn by God Himself. Hebrews 10:20 explains: "By a new and living way, which he hath consecrated for us, through the veil, that is to say, his flesh." Christ's body was torn on the cross, and at that moment the veil was torn. The way stood open.

**The Tabernacle, the Son of God Dwelling Among Us**

Brothers and sisters, the heart of chapter 26 is not measurements but a verb: God will "dwell" among His people. The Hebrew is shakan, from which the rabbis drew the word Shekinah. When John 1:14 says "the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us," the Greek verb eskenosen literally means "He pitched His tent among us." John chose that word deliberately. He wanted every reader of the Old Testament to remember this chapter.

The tent I raised in the wilderness was only a shadow. The true Tabernacle is the body of Christ. God does not finally dwell in acacia wood and gold; He dwells in the incarnate Son. And now, by His Spirit, Christ dwells in every believer and in His church. You are the temple of God, and the Spirit dwells in you (1 Cor. 3:16). This chapter is not about an ancient tent. It is about you.

## 历史背景（中文）

出埃及记二十五至三十一章是神颁布会幕建造指示的完整单元，学者称之为"会幕法典"（Tabernacle Code）。这段经文与创世记一至二章有深刻的文学平行：神七次对摩西说话（25:1; 30:11,17,22,34; 31:1,12），呼应创世记七日的创造结构，暗示会幕是神在以色列中间重建"伊甸园同在"的新创造。

从考古和文化背景看，古近东的神庙建筑通常是君王彰显自身荣耀的纪念碑，而会幕恰恰相反：结构可移动，材料来自百姓的奉献，目的是神同在于子民。这与当时埃及、迦南的神庙文化形成鲜明对比。

第二十六章所述的幔子工艺（青色紫色朱红色捻线，绣基路伯）与当时已知的古代近东纺织工艺高度吻合，反映了出埃及前以色列人在埃及学到的精湛手艺，神借着以色列人从埃及带出的财物（12:35-36）来建造祂的居所，历史的吊诡，也是神的主权。

## Historical Context (English)

Exodus 25–31 forms a unified literary unit scholars call the "Tabernacle Code." Notably, God speaks to Moses seven times within this section (25:1; 30:11, 17, 22, 34; 31:1, 12), paralleling the seven days of creation in Genesis 1–2. This literary parallel suggests the Tabernacle represents a new creation, God re-establishing His Eden-presence in the midst of Israel.

Archaeologically, ancient Near Eastern temples were typically royal monuments glorifying the king who built them. The Tabernacle inverts this pattern: it was portable, constructed from the freewill offerings of the people, and its purpose was entirely relational, God dwelling among His covenant people. This stands in sharp contrast to the immovable temples of Egypt and Canaan.

The embroidery techniques described in chapter 26, twisted fine linen with blue, purple, and scarlet thread, correspond well to textile arts documented in ancient Egypt, where Israel had labored for generations. Providentially, the gold, silver, and fine fabrics Israel had "plundered" from Egypt (12:35-36) became the material of God's dwelling place. Egypt's wealth, extracted as restitution from the house of slavery, was consecrated to the house of worship.

## 经文结构（中文）

出埃及记第二十六章结构清晰：

**一、内层幔子（1-6节）**
十幅细麻幔子，每五幅一组，用蓝色金钩连接。

**二、外层山羊毛幔子（7-13节）**
十一幅山羊毛幔子，每六幅、五幅各一组，用铜钩连接；多出的半幅垂在会幕后面。

**三、最外层遮盖（14节）**
红色公羊皮盖在外面，海狗皮为最外层。

**四、竖板与银座（15-30节）**
皂荚木竖板包金，南面、北面各二十块，西面六块另加两块角板；每块立在两个银座上。

**五、隔幔与门幔（31-37节）**
隔开圣所与至圣所的幔子，悬挂在四根金包柱子上；门口的帘子挂在五根柱子上。

全章按"由内而外"的逻辑展开，反映神学原则：神的同在是核心，一切外部结构都是为保护和导向这神圣的中心。

## Structure (English)

Exodus 26 is organized concentrically, moving from the innermost beauty outward:

**I. Inner Linen Curtains (vv. 1–6)**
Ten curtains of fine twisted linen with cherubim, joined in two sets of five by fifty golden clasps.

**II. Outer Goat-Hair Curtains (vv. 7–13)**
Eleven curtains of goat hair, joined by fifty bronze clasps; the extra half-curtain hung at the rear.

**III. Outermost Coverings (v. 14)**
A layer of ram skins dyed red, topped by sea cow hides, plain, protective, unadorned.

**IV. Upright Frames and Silver Bases (vv. 15–30)**
Forty-eight acacia-wood frames overlaid with gold: twenty for the south, twenty for the north, six plus two corner frames for the west. Each stood in two silver sockets.

**V. The Inner Veil and Entrance Screen (vv. 31–37)**
The veil separating Holy Place from Most Holy Place, hung on four gold-overlaid pillars; the entrance screen hung on five pillars.

The structure moves "from glory outward", the most holy things at the center, with each successive layer less ornate. Access to God is graduated, reserved, and prescribed.

## 神学要点（中文）

第二十六章的神学核心是**神的超越与内住**（Divine Transcendence and Immanence）的张力。

**神的圣洁与隔离（超越性）：** 幔子的设置不是装饰，而是神学宣告，神是圣洁的，罪人不能随便进入祂的同在。隔幔绣着基路伯，守卫着至圣所，正如基路伯曾守卫伊甸园的进入（创3:24）。人类因罪失去了与神的直接同在，这是严肃的宣告。

**神的恩典与俯就（内住性）：** 然而神却主动要"住在以色列人中间"（出25:8）。会幕的存在本身就是恩典，是神主权的拣选，是神单方面的恩典，使有罪的百姓可以在规定的方式下亲近祂。这不是百姓的功德，而是神的旨意。

**银座的神学：** 整个会幕的竖板立在银座上，而银来自赎罪的钱（出30:11-16）。改革宗神学强调：与神同在的根基是赎罪，不是人的宗教表现。约翰·加尔文注释说，会幕的每一细节都指向神圣洁的本性，同时也指向祂预备赦免之道的恩典。

**礼仪的意义：** 神严格规定会幕的样式，表明真正的敬拜必须照着神所启示的方式进行，而不是人自己创造的方式。改革宗"唯独圣经"原则（Sola Scriptura）的含义之一，就是敬拜的规范原则（Regulative Principle of Worship）：只有神命令的才在敬拜中被允许。

## Theology (English)

The central theological tension of chapter 26 is the interplay between **Divine Transcendence and Immanence**.

**Transcendence, God's Holiness and Separation:** The veil and the graduated structure of the Tabernacle declare that God is holy and cannot be approached casually by sinners. The cherubim embroidered on the veil echo the cherubim placed at Eden's entrance after the Fall (Gen. 3:24), guarding the presence of God from unholy intrusion. The architecture itself is a sermon on the holiness of God.

**Immanence, God's Grace in Condescension:** Yet the very existence of the Tabernacle is an act of grace. God said, "Let them make Me a sanctuary, that I may dwell in their midst" (Ex. 25:8). This initiative was entirely divine. A holy God choosing to dwell among a sinful people is not self-evident; it is astonishing, sovereign mercy.

**The Theology of the Silver Sockets:** The forty-eight frames of the Tabernacle stood in silver sockets, and that silver came from the ransom payment of Israel's men (Ex. 30:11–16). The entire structure of God's dwelling rested on atonement money. As Calvin observed, every detail of the Tabernacle pointed simultaneously to God's holiness and to His provision of a way of forgiveness.

**The Regulative Principle:** God's insistence on an exact pattern (25:40; 26:30) reflects what Reformed theology calls the Regulative Principle of Worship: true worship is governed by Scripture alone, not by human invention. We may not approach God on our own terms.

## 基督的影子（中文）

第二十六章的基督预表丰富而深刻：

**幔子，基督的身体：** 希伯来书十章20节明确解释，至圣所的幔子象征基督的身体。当耶稣在十字架上断气，圣殿的幔子从上到下裂为两半（马太福音27:51），这是神亲手撕开的！那撕裂的幔子宣告：通往至圣所的道路，通过基督的受死，向所有信徒永远开放了。人类自亚当以来第一次，可以凭借基督的血"坦然无惧地来到施恩的宝座前"（希伯来书4:16）。

**会幕，道成肉身：** 约翰福音一章14节用"住在我们中间"（字面义：搭幕于我们中间）来描述基督的降生。会幕是基督降世的预表，神在帐幕里与人同住，正如神子在肉身里与人同住。正如会幕外表朴素，基督降生也是卑微的；正如会幕内里是神的荣光，基督里面充满了恩典与真理。

**大祭司，基督的中保：** 进入至圣所的专属通道只为大祭司保留。基督是我们永远的大祭司（希伯来书7:24-25），不是每年一次带着动物的血进入至圣所，而是一次永远地，带着自己的血进入了天上真正的至圣所，为我们的罪做了完全的赎罪祭。

**旷野中的会幕，末世的应许：** 启示录二十一章3节引用会幕的语言描述新天新地："神的帐幕在人间，祂要与人同住。"会幕是应许的开始，基督是应许的成全，新创造是应许的最终完成。

## Christ's Shadow (English)

Chapter 26 is extraordinarily rich in Christological typology:

**The Veil, Christ's Body:** Hebrews 10:20 explicitly interprets the inner veil as a type of Christ's flesh. When Jesus died on the cross, the Temple veil was torn in two from top to bottom (Matt. 27:51), torn by God Himself, from above. That act declared: through the death of Christ, the way into the Most Holy Place is now open to all believers. For the first time since Eden, sinners may "draw near with confidence to the throne of grace" (Heb. 4:16).

**The Tabernacle, The Incarnation:** John 1:14 deliberately echoes Tabernacle language: "The Word became flesh and dwelt [literally: tabernacled] among us." As the Tabernacle was God's dwelling in the midst of Israel, so the Incarnation was God's dwelling in human flesh. As the Tabernacle appeared plain from without but housed the shekinah glory within, so Christ "had no form or majesty that we should look at him" (Isa. 53:2), yet was "full of grace and truth."

**The Restricted Access, Christ the Only Way:** The Most Holy Place could only be entered by the High Priest, once a year, with blood. This typifies Christ's unique mediatorial role. He is the one High Priest who entered the true heavenly sanctuary "once for all" with His own blood (Heb. 9:12), securing eternal redemption for His people.

**The Portable Tabernacle, Eschatological Promise:** The movable Tabernacle anticipated the ultimate fulfillment in Revelation 21:3: "Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man. He will dwell with them." The Tabernacle was the beginning; Christ is the fulfillment; the New Creation is the consummation of God's promise to dwell with His people forever.
